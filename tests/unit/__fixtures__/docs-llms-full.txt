# What is Helix Foundry?

Page type: Concept

Helix Foundry turns the tools your company runs on into one connected graph. Connect
sources such as Stripe or your PostgreSQL database, send it your app's or agent's own
data, upload the documents your tables don't cover, let Foundry's agent draft an
ontology across them, then load it into a Helix database, ask it questions, and
explore it as a graph or as records. Connectors keep syncing on a schedule, and
ontologies refresh when their data changes.

# Use Foundry from your agent

Page type: Guide

Foundry's tools are on the Helix MCP, at `https://mcp.helix-db.com/mcp`. Connect your
agent to it, sign in once, and the agent works in Foundry as you: it reads your
ontologies, their versions, loads and graph, asks them questions, and makes the changes
you ask for, each after a confirmation. It can't add a connector's credentials, delete
anything or change billing; those stay on the website.
