# Master services agreement

This agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on 1 October 2026 between Acme (“we”), Globex Corporation and Initech LLC (each a “Customer”).

## 1. Services

We provide our analytics platform to Globex for €48,000 a year, invoiced quarterly through Stripe. Initech pays €12,500 a year for the same service — invoiced monthly.

## 2. Referral

Umbrella Holdings introduced the parties and receives a referral fee of 5 % of the first year’s fees.

## 3. Term

The Agreement runs for 24 months and renews for 12 months unless a party gives 90 days’ notice.
